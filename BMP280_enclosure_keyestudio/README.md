# Enclosure for the Keyestudio BMP280 sensor module

Tiny 3d-printed enclosure for the [Keyestudio BMP280](https://www.keyestudio.com/products/keyestudio-bmp280-module-digital-sensor-temperature-humidity-barometric-pressure-sensor-module).

> [!NOTE]
> The manufacturer did not provide any dimensions. I had to take measurements myself from one of the modules I had.
> After printing the enclosure for multiple sensors, I noticed that many PCBs are slightly different in size.
> This could lead to the enclosure not fitting perfectly or not being able to close properly.
